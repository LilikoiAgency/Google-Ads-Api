Big Bully Turf organic traffic, October 2026

Source: GA4 Traffic acquisition report, Total users by Session primary channel group, October 1–31, 2026. Totals are unique users for the month. The daily chart counts users per day, so a person who visits on several days appears on each of those days.

What each channel means
Organic Search: visitors who clicked an unpaid result on Google, Bing or another search engine.
Direct: visitors who typed the web address, used a bookmark, or came from a link Google Analytics couldn't trace, such as one in a text message or app.
Referral: visitors who clicked a link to the site from another website.
Organic Social: visitors who clicked an unpaid post or profile link on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or another social platform.
Other organic: smaller unpaid sources combined: free shopping listings (Organic Shopping), unpaid video such as YouTube (Organic Video), email links (Email), and AI tools like ChatGPT (AI Assistant).
